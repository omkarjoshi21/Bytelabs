## Project description

Designed and developed the ByteLabs website to introduce my independent
software studio, explain its services, and showcase project work.

The website combines responsive layouts, a consistent brand identity, subtle
animations, a founder profile, and a VedCare showcase with feature details and
screenshots. Contact options connect visitors through email and WhatsApp.

Built with React, Vite, and CSS, deployed on Render, with search metadata,
structured data, and a sitemap submitted through Google Search Console.

My role: Founder; website design, front-end development, testing, deployment,
and search setup.

Live website: https://bytelabs-1.onrender.com/
